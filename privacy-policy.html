Pixels Under Par Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pixels Under Par collects, uses, and protects information when you play the game. Pixels Under Par is developed by an independent developer.

Information We Collect

Pixels Under Par may store gameplay data such as progress, unlocked items, selected cosmetics, scores, leaderboard entries, and your chosen player name.

If you choose to sign in to protect your save, Pixels Under Par uses Unity Player Accounts and Unity Authentication. Unity may process account information such as your Unity Player Account identifier and email address as part of the sign-in flow.

How We Use Information

We use game data to save your progress, restore your save across installs or devices where supported, display leaderboard entries, and provide normal game features.

Third-Party Services

Pixels Under Par uses Unity Gaming Services, including Unity Authentication, Unity Player Accounts, Unity Cloud Save, Unity Leaderboards, Unity Remote Config, Unity Analytics, and Unity LevelPlay advertising. These services are provided by Unity Technologies and are subject to Unity's own privacy terms and policies. LevelPlay may use additional advertising partners to provide an ad.

The iOS game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present advertising privacy messages and manage consent choices. Google processes information needed to provide these forms, as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising and Tracking

Pixels Under Par may show occasional interstitial ads using Unity LevelPlay. Advertising services may process device information, IP address, advertising identifiers where permitted, app interactions, approximate location derived from an IP address, and ad interaction or performance information to deliver, limit, secure, and measure ads.

Before requesting ads, the iOS game checks your advertising privacy status with Google UMP and displays any required privacy forms. Your choices are passed to supported advertising partners through Unity LevelPlay. Where required, you can review or withdraw consent using Ad Privacy in Options. If a privacy check fails and UMP does not permit ad requests, the game continues without requesting ads. Choosing non-personalised ads does not prevent all processing of device or advertising information.

Cross-app tracking on supported Apple devices requires Apple's tracking permission separately from advertising consent. Denying consent or tracking permission does not prevent you from playing, including offline. The game also sends a do-not-sell-or-share signal to supported advertising partners.

Cloud Saves and Leaderboards

If cloud saving is available, your save data may be stored using Unity Cloud Save. Leaderboard entries may show your selected player name and score or progress information.

Data Sharing

We do not directly sell your personal information. Data may be processed by Unity Gaming Services and advertising partners as needed to provide account, cloud save, leaderboard, advertising, fraud prevention, and related game functionality. The game sends supported privacy opt-out signals to LevelPlay before requesting ads.

Children's Privacy

Pixels Under Par is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where parental consent is required by law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Your Choices

You can play without linking a Unity Player Account, but local save data may be lost if the app is deleted. You can sign out from the profile screen. The Options screen lets you review or change your advertising choice, open this policy, and report an inappropriate or age-inappropriate ad. You may also request data access or deletion using the contact details below.
