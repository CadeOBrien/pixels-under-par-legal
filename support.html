Pixels Under Par Support

Need help with the game? Contact cade.obrien10@gmail.com.

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS version, game version, and what happened. Screenshots and steps to reproduce the problem are helpful. Never send passwords or sign-in codes.

How do I play?

Drag on the play area to aim and set your shot strength, then release to shoot. You can invert the shot direction in Options. Try to finish each hole in as few strokes as possible.

Can I play offline?

Classic and Endless are available offline. Online features, including cloud synchronization and leaderboard updates, need an internet connection.

How do I protect my progress?

Use the profile screen to link a Unity Player Account for cloud saves. Without a linked account, deleting the app can permanently remove local progress. If progress appears missing, check that you are using the same account and contact support before resetting your save or reinstalling.

How do I change advertising privacy choices?

Open Options and use Ad Privacy when it is available. You can also manage Apple's tracking permission in your device's Settings. Advertising privacy choices do not prevent you from playing.

How do I report an inappropriate ad?

Use the ad-report option in Options, or email support with the approximate time, advertiser name, and a screenshot if available.

Privacy and data requests

For questions about your information or to request data access or deletion, email support. See the Privacy Policy for details about the game's services and data use.
